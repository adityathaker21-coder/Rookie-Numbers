Nobody teaches this stuff in school

More than half the country now lets high school athletes earn money off their name. That's great. But nobody walks them through the financial side. Not their school. Not their coach.

The financial literacy classes that exist don't touch 1099 income or self-employment tax. And most NIL resources are made for college athletes who already have agents.

We started Rookie Numbers because we kept watching friends sign things without reading them. Somebody had to start talking about this, so we did.

How it works

We come to your school and run a workshop. It's not a lecture. Here's the rough shape of it.

1

We set it up with your school

A coach, athletic director, or student org reaches out. We figure out a time that works, how many athletes, and what to focus on.

2

We run the workshop

45 minutes to an hour. We go through taxes, contracts, budgeting, and valuation with real examples. We answer questions the whole time.

3

Athletes leave knowing the basics

By the end, they know what 1099 income is, how to read a basic contract, and how to figure out if a deal is worth taking.
