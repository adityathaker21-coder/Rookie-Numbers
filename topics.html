What we actually go over

Stuff you'd want to know before a check clears. We skip the jargon and focus on what's practical.

01

Taxes on NIL income

NIL money is 1099 income. You owe federal taxes, state taxes, and self-employment tax on it. Most kids don't find that out until they've already spent the full amount.

02

Reading a contract

Exclusivity clauses, image usage rights, payment timelines. We go through the parts of a deal that trip people up and what to push back on.

03

Budgeting irregular income

You might get paid $500 one month and nothing the next three. We talk about how to handle that without blowing through everything in week one.

04

Knowing your value

Some brands will offer you $100 for something worth $2,000. We go over how to figure out what a deal is actually worth and when to say no.

05

Protecting your name

Your name and image are yours. Some contracts try to change that. We look at what to watch for in licensing and usage terms so you don't give away more than you meant to.

06

Picking the right deals

Not every offer is a good fit. Some pay well but hurt your reputation. Some look small but open better doors later. We talk through how to think about that.
